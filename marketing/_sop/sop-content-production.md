# SOP — Content & Creative Production

## 1. Creative Production Workflow

1. **Select product(s) and angle** — based on what needs promotion (new offer, slow-moving stock, seasonal push) and rotate hooks per `sop-meta-ads-campaign-management.md` Section 7.
2. **Take/select product photography** — close-up macro shots preferred, natural lighting, minimal props (rosemary, citrus, peppers used sparingly).
3. **Build in Canva using Magic Layers**, based on the existing template (see `brand-style-guide.md` for exact element placement: logo top-left, hook text, price badge bottom-left, CTA button bottom-center, trust icons bottom-right, WhatsApp number + delivery areas in bottom strip).
4. **Swap only:** hook text, product image, and price. Keep the layout, logo position, and CTA identical across creatives to preserve brand consistency and allow clean hook testing.
5. **Check watermark placement** — reposition or lower opacity if it overlaps/obscures the product itself.
6. **Write caption** per `brand-voice-guide.md` — warm tone, core messaging pillars from `brand-content.md`, WhatsApp CTA with number.
7. **Publish as organic Page post first**, then follow `sop-meta-ads-campaign-management.md` Section 2 to turn it into an ad.

## 2. Caption Structure (standard template)

```
[Hook / headline — emoji optional]

[1-2 line supporting sentence — benefit or emotional angle]

✅ [Trust signal 1 — e.g. جودة ونظافة عالية]
✅ [Trust signal 2 — e.g. الدفع عند الاستلام]
🛵 [Delivery areas]
🎁 [Free delivery threshold, if relevant]

📲 اطلب دلوقتي على الواتساب: +90 534 570 30 37

#ملحمة_القدس #لحوم_طازجة #باشاك_شهير #اسنيورت #ارناؤوط_كوي #عرب_اسطنبول
```

## 3. Hook Bank (rotate for testing)

- Price/value angle: "أسعار منافسة" / specific discount callout
- Urgency angle: "الكمية محدودة" / time-bound offer
- Direct/action angle: "اطلب دلوقتي وتوصلك بكرة"
- Trust/freshness angle: "اللون ده بيحكي كل حاجة" / "الطازج بيتكلم"
- Craftsmanship angle: "قطّعناها بإيدينا مخصوص ليك"
- Occasion angle: mangal/grill-focused for Friday/family gatherings

## 4. Content Types & Cadence

| Type | Priority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reels/Stories | Primary | Behind-the-scenes cutting/packaging (no face), product close-ups |
| Product catalog carousel | Secondary | Multiple products, prices, used for broader awareness |
| Discount/promo single post | Secondary | One product, one clear price/offer, strongest for direct conversion testing |

## 5. Quality Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Logo visible and unobstructed (top-left)
- [ ] Price badge accurate and current (cross-check with Enes — see `product-offerings.md` live pricing note)
- [ ] Watermark does not obscure product
- [ ] CTA button and WhatsApp number both present and correct
- [ ] Trust signals (cash on delivery, delivery areas) included
- [ ] No mention of market sourcing anywhere in copy or design
- [ ] Arabic text renders correctly right-to-left
- [ ] Masculine verb forms used when referring to Enes

## 6. Video Voiceover Production (Haitham character)

1. Script written per product list, timed per segment (e.g., 2 seconds per product for a fast-cut multi-product video).
2. Include bracketed sound-effect/tone cues for the voice actor (e.g., knife sound, sizzle, whoosh transition) directly in the script.
3. Deliver a clean, unformatted version of the script (no headers/bold) to the voice actor for recording — formatting is only for internal reference.
