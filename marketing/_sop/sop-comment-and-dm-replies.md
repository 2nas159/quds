# SOP — Comment & DM Reply Handling

## 1. General Reply Principles

- Always warm, professional tone — never defensive or evasive, even when a comment is skeptical or slightly rude.
- Always redirect to WhatsApp (+90 534 570 30 37) for anything requiring a specific answer (price, order, availability).
- Never mention sourcing from a market — see hard rule in `brand-content.md`.
- Use masculine verb forms when writing as/about Enes.
- Personalize where possible — reference the specific product/question asked rather than a fully generic copy-paste reply.

## 2. Pricing Questions — Reply Framework

**Do not** say prices are hidden/secret or give a reply that feels like a dismissal.

**Do** give a real reason (price depends on weight/cut, or updates regularly for best pricing) and route to WhatsApp with a promise of a fast reply.

**Approved reference reply:**
> "اهلا وسهلا يا فندم، إحنا شغالين بنظام التوصيل المباشر والدفع عند الاستلام من غير محل للزيارة لتقليل التكاليف علينا والاسعار عليكم — بنجهزلك طلبك طازة ونوصله لباب بيتك. لاي استفسارات اخري تقدري حضرتك تتواصلي مع الفريق من هنا: +90 534 570 30 37"

**When a specific product price is known and shareable, state it directly:**
> "[Product] الكيلو بـ[price] ليرة [emoji]، وللطلب أو معرفة أسعار باقي الأصناف، تقدر تتواصل معانا على الواتساب: +90 534 570 30 37"

## 3. "Where is the store?" / "Can I visit?" Questions

**Approved reference reply (Arabic):**
> "أهلاً بيك! إحنا Dark Store يعني بندبح ونجهز الطلب ونوصله لباب بيتك مباشرة، مفيش محل تزوره. بس تقدر تطلب وتستلم بمنطقتك على طول 😊 واتساب: +90 534 570 30 37"

Never reveal a physical sourcing location. Frame the no-storefront model as a deliberate choice that lowers costs and benefits the customer (see reply framework above).

## 4. Halal / Trust / Origin Questions (Arabic & Turkish)

Reassure with hygiene, in-house butchering, and control — never mention external sourcing (market or otherwise beyond "our own facility").

**Approved reference reply (Turkish):**
> "Elbette, sorunuz için teşekkürler 🙏 Etlerimizi kendi kesimhanemizde, kontrollü ve hijyenik şartlarda biz kendimiz kesiyoruz. Gerçek kuzu/koyun etidir. Detaylı bilgi için WhatsApp'tan bize ulaşabilirsiniz: +90 534 570 30 37"

## 5. Delivery Area / Fee Questions

- Delivery areas: Başakşehir, Esenyurt, Arnavutköy, Bahçeşehir
- Free delivery: orders over 5,000 TRY
- Delivery fee under 5,000 TRY: 150 TRY
- If asked in Turkish, reply in Turkish; if Arabic, reply in Arabic. Match the customer's language.

## 6. Misunderstood / Ambiguous Questions

- If a term is ambiguous (e.g., a cut name that could mean different things), acknowledge politely and clarify rather than guessing — e.g., apologize for the mix-up and offer to confirm exact pricing on WhatsApp once the specific product is clear.

## 7. Escalation

- Any comment implying a serious complaint, health/safety concern, or repeated dissatisfaction from the same customer should be flagged to Enes directly rather than handled with a standard template reply.
