## Findings (sourced)

### 1. How the Istanbul / Turkey market packages and promotes Ramadan meat offers

**Turkish quick-commerce and grocery (Migros, and by extension the A101/BİM/ŞOK/Carrefour cluster)**
- Migros ran an explicit **Ramadan 2026 campaign from 28 January to 26 March 2026** — i.e. the campaign opened **~3 weeks before Ramadan started** and ran through to just after Eid. ([onurakay.com.tr](https://www.onurakay.com.tr/haber/migros-ramazan-2026-kampanyasi-basladi-300-urunde-fiyat-sabitlendi-dana-kiyma-ve-kusbasi-ne-kadar), [yenisokegazetesi.com](https://yenisokegazetesi.com/haber/27467416/migrostan-ramazan-kampanyasi-kolilerde-yuzde-40-tasarruf-300-urunde-sabit-fiyat))
- Core mechanic was **price-fixing / price-lock on staples**: ~300 own-brand essentials held at a fixed price for the whole month, plus ~3,000 discounted lines. Red meat was singled out — **dana kıyma (ground beef) fixed at 750 TL/kg and dana kuşbaşı (cubed beef) at 850 TL/kg** for the duration, explicitly framed as "price stability in red meat, a key ingredient for the iftar table." ([investing.com](https://tr.investing.com/news/economy-news/migros-ramazan-ay-boyunca-migros-markal-yaklask-300-temel-urunun-fiyatn-sabitledi-3764241), [onurakay.com.tr](https://www.onurakay.com.tr/haber/migros-ramazan-2026-kampanyasi-basladi-300-urunde-fiyat-sabitlendi-dana-kiyma-ve-kusbasi-ne-kadar))
- **Ramadan "koli" (bundle boxes)** priced at **499.95 TL and 799.95 TL**, marketed as "up to 40% saving versus buying items individually." These are mostly dry-goods erzak boxes (oil, rice, bulgur, pasta, tea, sugar, legumes, canned protein) rather than meat bundles. ([yenisokegazetesi.com](https://yenisokegazetesi.com/haber/27467416/migrostan-ramazan-kampanyasi-kolilerde-yuzde-40-tasarruf-300-urunde-sabit-fiyat), [gzt.com](https://www.gzt.com/gundem/2026-ramazan-kolisi-fiyatlari-ne-kadar-sok-market-ramazan-paketi-icerigi-4016278))
- A **donation / "sponsor a table" mechanic** is near-universal: Migros "Bir Sofra da Sen Kur" with the Turkish Red Crescent offered a **240 TL iftar menu** (soup, main, side, dessert) delivered to people in need. This charitable add-on is a standard part of Ramadan retail campaigns in Turkey. ([yenisokegazetesi.com](https://yenisokegazetesi.com/haber/27467416/migrostan-ramazan-kampanyasi-kolilerde-yuzde-40-tasarruf-300-urunde-sabit-fiyat))
- Marketplace platforms (Trendyol, Hepsiburada, Çiçeksepeti, n11) run dedicated **"Ramazan Paketi / Ramazan Kolisi" category pages** live well before Ramadan, with **small / medium / large tiers** ("her bütçeye uygun") and item counts advertised as the value cue (12 to 55 items). ([trendyol.com](https://www.trendyol.com/ramazan-kurban-paketi-x-c144889), [hepsiburada.com](https://www.hepsiburada.com/ramazan-paketi-c-28001320), [ciceksepeti.com](https://www.ciceksepeti.com/d/ramazan-paketleri))

**Online butchers / kasap delivery (Afiyet Et Kasap, Online Kasap, Kasaptan Sipariş, KasaptanAl, Kaasap, Dilek Gurme)**
- These players lead year-round with **"doğal / katkısız / çiftlikten sofraya" (natural / additive-free / farm-to-table)** and **cold-chain (soğuk zincir, frigorifik araç) delivery within ~24 hours**, plus a **free-shipping threshold** (Afiyet: 800 TL; others commonly 150 TL+ for pantry, higher for meat). ([afiyet.com.tr](https://www.afiyet.com.tr/ramazan-paketi_139), [onlinekasap.com](https://onlinekasap.com/), [kasaptansiparis.com](https://www.kasaptansiparis.com/home))
- **"Mangal paketi" (BBQ/grill bundle)** is the established bundle format these butchers already sell, and it is the closest analogue to an iftar meat bundle. Example composition (Tarsan Çiftlik mangal paketi): **1 kg butcher's köfte, 1 kg lamb şiş, 1 kg lamb pirzola (chops), 0.5 kg halloumi, 900 g sheep's yoghurt, plus tomato / onion / cucumber / çarliston pepper (0.5 kg each) and 1 pack pide.** ([tarsanciftlik.com](https://www.tarsanciftlik.com/alisveris/mangal-paketİ))
- Standard **per-head portion math** used by Turkish sellers for a 4-person grill bundle: lamb chops 1.5 kg, köfte-mince 1 kg, chicken wings/thighs 1.2 kg, sucuk 600 g, lamb şiş (cubed) 800 g — i.e. roughly **250–400 g meat per person** across cuts. Useful anchor for "family of X" sizing. ([ibrahiminyeri.com](https://www.ibrahiminyeri.com/post/et-mangal-kisi-basi-porsiyon-hesabi-maliyet))
- Afiyet Et publishes a page literally titled **"Ramazan Paketi"** — the format is expected by the market — though current contents/pricing were not populated at time of research. ([afiyet.com.tr](https://www.afiyet.com.tr/ramazan-paketi_139))
- Ready-to-cook convenience SKUs (e.g. Online Kasap "hazır pişirmelik kuzu tandır 1700 g") are pushed hard in the Ramadan window as the "no prep, just put it in the oven" option. ([onlinekasap.com](https://onlinekasap.com/urun/hazir-pisirmelik-kuzu-tandir-1700gr/))

**Arabic-community-focused butchers in Istanbul (e.g. ملحمة الهلال / Al-Hilal Meat, ملحمة و قصاب المدينة)**
- Positioning is **Arabic-language, "authentic taste," 24/7 support, fast delivery, own cutting**, with named delivery zones that overlap ours heavily — Al-Hilal lists **Esenyurt, Kıraç, Talatpaşa, İnönü, Yeşilkent, Yakuplu, Beylikdüzü, Bahçeşehir, Avcılar.** ([alheelal-meat.com](https://www.alheelal-meat.com/), [alheelal-meat.com/about-us](https://www.alheelal-meat.com/about-us))
- They advertise **daily-changing offers** ("عروض بشكل يومي") rather than a fixed published Ramadan bundle — so there is a **visible gap**: no Arabic-community butcher in our zones is currently owning a clearly packaged, clearly priced "Iftar bundle" concept in market-facing content. This is an angle open to us.
- These competitors do use **grilled-meat / prepared-meat imagery** and family framing, but their trust-building is thin (mostly "farm to table" claims) — our behind-the-scenes / in-house-butchering content is a stronger trust play if executed.

### 2. Ramadan 2027 timing → campaign shape

- **Ramadan 2027 starts ~7 February 2027; Eid al-Fitr ~8–9 March 2027.**
- Retail precedent (Migros opened 28 Jan for a 17 Feb Ramadan, ~3 weeks early) implies our **pre-Ramadan stock-up push should open ~third week of January 2027 (roughly 20–26 Jan), 2 weeks before Ramadan.**
- Two distinct buying behaviours to design for:
  1. **Pre-Ramadan stock-up week** (~last week of January into first days of February): larger, freezer-oriented "prep once" order. Higher basket value, lower frequency.
  2. **Weekly through-Ramadan buying** (each week of February to early March): smaller top-up orders for that week's iftars and any عزومة (hosting night). Higher frequency, lower basket.
- MENA advertising guidance is consistent that **food ad delivery should be weighted to the hours between sunset (iftar) and suhoor**, and daytime fasting-hours food messaging underperforms / can irritate. ([mcgmasters.com](https://www.mcgmasters.com/blog-posts/ramadan-campaign-strategy-for-the-middle-east), [videngrowth.com](https://videngrowth.com/blog/ramadan-advertising-guide))
- Demand spikes again in the **last 10 days and pre-Eid** (hosting, Eid lunch) — worth a second creative beat rather than letting the campaign fade after week one. ([zawya.com — Yango Ads Ramadan 2026 playbook](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/yango-ads-reveals-ramadan-2026-demand-playbook-for-mena-brands-sfdzdqra))

### 4. What Arabic-speaking communities in Istanbul want at Ramadan

- Ramadan is strongly **nostalgia- and tradition-driven** for Syrian and Egyptian families in Istanbul — they actively try to recreate "Ramadan as they knew it back home." Meat-heavy hosting nights (عزومات) are central. ([sana.sy](https://sana.sy/syria-and-the-world/2413882/), [elwatannews.com](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8230586))
- Recurring Ramadan meat dishes across Egyptian/Syrian/Levantine tables and the cuts they map to:
  - **طواجن / أوزي / لحمة بالفرن** → lamb leg (فخذ), shoulder (كتف), neck (رقبة), bone-in stew pieces (لحم قطع بالعظم)
  - **مشاوي / كباب حلة / عزومة** → lamb ribs/chops (ريش), cubed lamb (لحم قطع), kofta (كفتة), sausage (سجق / سجق مصري)
  - **محاشي / كوسا وورق عنب / بطاطس محشية** → ground meat (مفروم), ideally a leaner blend
  - **شوربة رمضان (لسان عصفور / فريكة / شوربة خضار)** → soup bones / neck / oxtail (عظم للشوربة، رقبة، عكاوي)
  - **فتة / كوارع / رأس** and **سحور** → liver (كبدة), tripe (كرشة / ممبار), heart, sausage
- Practical needs: **buy once for the week/freezer**, **portion by family size**, **familiar cuts named in Arabic** (Turkish cut names and butchery conventions differ and cause friction), delivery that **fits around fasting** (evening or scheduled), and **cash on delivery** (already our default, and a trust asset here).
- Al-Hilal's zone list confirms a **dense, delivery-primed Arabic customer base** in exactly our streets. ([alheelal-meat.com/about-us](https://www.alheelal-meat.com/about-us))

### 5. Creative / format trends for Ramadan food promos (Instagram / Facebook, this region)

- **Short video (Reels) is the priority format.** Reels get more engagement than feed ads and ~40% lower CPMs; first **3 seconds decide** watch-through; strong on-screen text + explicit CTA needed. ([candavarci.com.tr](https://candavarci.com.tr/blog/dijital-pazarlama/instagram-reklam-rehberi), [metaydijitalmarketing.com](https://www.metaydijitalmarketing.com/instagram-reels-reklam-stratejileri/))
- **Creative fatigue is fast** — same asset starts tiring in **7–10 days**; plan **8–12 fresh creatives across the month**, rotated into the existing ad set (consistent with our "add creative, don't pause" rule). ([candavarci.com.tr](https://candavarci.com.tr/blog/dijital-pazarlama/instagram-reklam-rehberi))
- **Tone that worked in Ramadan 2026:** warm, relatable, slightly humorous, "authentic / familiar flavours," anti-glossy-spectacle. Togetherness, family, gratitude, community. Purely commercial messages underperformed storytelling. ([thinkmarketingmagazine.com](https://thinkmarketingmagazine.com/ramadan-2026-the-campaigns-that-truly-stood-out/), [mcgmasters.com](https://www.mcgmasters.com/blog-posts/ramadan-campaign-strategy-for-the-middle-east), [dmidigitalmarketing.com](https://dmidigitalmarketing.com/10-evergreen-ramadan-content-ideas-for-food-brands-on-social-media/))

([videngrowth.com](https://videngrowth.com/blog/ramadan-advertising-guide), [dmidigitalmarketing.com](https://dmidigitalmarketing.com/10-evergreen-ramadan-content-ideas-for-food-brands-on-social-media/))

## So What — Recommendations for our campaign

Recommendations only. Final promo/pricing/targeting decisions sit with Enes and `campaign-strategist`.

### Bundle framing
- Package it as a **named, fixed-contents "Iftar table" meat bundle** in Arabic ("سفرة رمضان" / "عزومة رمضان" territory — final naming to `content-creator`). The market gap in our zones is a clearly-composed, clearly-sized, clearly-priced Arabic bundle; competitors sell loose cuts or dry-goods boxes, not this.
- Contents should map to **familiar Ramadan dishes named in Arabic**, not Turkish cut names: a mix covering طواجن/فرن (leg or shoulder pieces), مشاوي/عزومة (ribs or cubed lamb + kofta), محاشي (ground meat), and شوربة (soup bones / neck). Naming the dish next to the cut is the differentiator.
- Lead value message = **"one order, your whole week of iftars sorted"** (convenience + planning), with a soft secondary value cue ("cheaper than buying each cut separately"). Do **not** copy Migros-style "price-lock" language — our prices move daily and rule #2 forbids unconfirmed price claims; frame value as *bundle convenience*, not a frozen price.
- Add an optional **suhoor mini add-on** (mince / liver / sausage) — competitors ignore suhoor entirely.
- Keep the convenience hook about **time and calm** ("مش محتاج تقف في الزحمة قبل الأذان") — never about where meat comes from. Rule #1 (no market-sourcing implication) is easy to trip here; keep it on *your kitchen, your queue, your evening*, not *the market*.

### Sizing tiers
Offer **three tiers by household size**, using the ~250–400 g-per-person planning norm (exact weights for Enes to finalise against stock and cost):
- **صغيرة — لعيلة صغيرة (3–4 أفراد):** ~2.5–3.5 kg mixed.
- **وسط — لعيلة (5–6 أفراد):** ~4.5–6 kg mixed. Position as the hero / default.
- **كبيرة — للعزومة (8–10 أفراد):** ~8–10 kg mixed. Target hosting nights and the last 10 days.
- Make at least the mid and large tier clear the **5,000 TRY free-delivery threshold** naturally, so free delivery becomes part of the bundle pitch.
- **Needs Enes to confirm:** exact cut mix, per-tier weights, whether ribs/leg are in stock at Ramadan volume, bundle price per tier, and whether a fixed bundle price can hold for a week at a time given daily cost movement.

### Positioning angle (primary → supporting)
1. **Primary: convenience for the occasion** — "your iftar table, planned and delivered, without the pre-adhan queue." Time, calm, one message on WhatsApp.
2. **Support: family / tradition / nostalgia** — recreating Ramadan "like back home," familiar cuts and dishes, the table everyone gathers at. This is what the Arabic community in Istanbul actively wants and what MENA creative rewarded in 2026.
3. **Support: trust** — in-house butchering, hygiene, cash on delivery, real behind-the-scenes. This is our structural advantage over Arabic-community competitors who only claim "farm to table."
4. **Optional, later: a light charity beat** ("sponsor a table") if Enes wants it — it is expected in this market, but only if it can be run credibly; do not fake it.

### Timing
- **Build week (early Jan 2027):** brief, copy, creatives ready.
- **Phase 1 — pre-Ramadan stock-up: ~20 Jan – 6 Feb 2027.** Push the "order once, freezer-ready for the first week" angle. Larger baskets.
- **Phase 2 — through Ramadan: ~7 Feb – 4 Mar 2027.** Weekly top-up angle; refresh creative every 7–10 days inside the same ad set (never pause it). Weight ad delivery and organic posts to **sunset–late evening**; pull back in fasting daylight hours.
- **Phase 3 — last 10 days / pre-Eid: ~5–9 Mar 2027.** Re-emphasise the large "عزومة" tier for hosting and Eid lunch.
- Avoid promo wording tied to the season ending, per growth-context guidance — the bundle should be retireable cleanly after Eid.

### Creative directions (hand to `content-creator`; Canva-only variants to `creative-designer`)
1. **"Unboxing the bundle" Reel** — hands only, opening the delivered package on the counter, naming each cut and the dish it's for ("دي للطاجن… دي للمشاوي… دي للشوربة"). Trust + planning in one. First 3 seconds: the box hitting the table + bold on-screen bundle name.
2. **Prep/process Reel** — in-house butchering and portioning the bundle, hygiene visible, no faces. On-screen text: توصيل سريع · الدفع عند الاستلام · طازج لباب بيتك. Cooking/prep process content over-indexes for engagement in Ramadan.
3. **"Countdown to iftar" short** — clock/light going to sunset, table filling with dishes made from the bundle, close on the WhatsApp CTA. Warm, familiar, slightly nostalgic tone — not glossy.
4. **Carousel: "بندل رمضان — اختار حجم عيلتك"** — one slide per tier (3–4 / 5–6 / 8–10), contents + "covers X iftars" + free delivery cue on the qualifying tiers. Cairo Bold for the tier name and size, brand red. Publish organically as a Page post first, then attach as existing-post ad (per the known Meta carousel + WhatsApp-objective workaround).
5. **"عزومة الليلة؟" Reel for the last 10 days** — the large bundle framed for hosting: big platter, plenty for guests, "one message and it's at your door tomorrow." Drives the high-basket tier when hosting peaks.

### Flags for Enes
- **Prices:** every price in this doc is an external market benchmark. Bundle pricing per tier must be set and confirmed by Enes; confirm whether a bundle price can be held stable for a week given daily cost movement.
- **Stock:** confirm Ramadan-volume availability of ribs (ريش), lamb leg/shoulder, and soup bones before these are named in a fixed bundle.
- **Charity beat:** only include a "sponsor a table" element if it can be delivered for real.
- **Dates:** Ramadan 2027 start (~7 Feb 2027) is subject to moon sighting — reconfirm in January.

### Suggested owners
