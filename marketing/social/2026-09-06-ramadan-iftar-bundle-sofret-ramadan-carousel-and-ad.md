## PART 1 — Instagram carousel (ORGANIC post)

- **Format:** Instagram carousel, 7 slides, 4:5 portrait
- **Goal:** communicate the whole bundle system at a glance — right-sized option for any household, what's in each tier, ordering is one WhatsApp message. Carries the *structure* + the *convenience/trust proof*.
- **Visual system:** `_templates/social-creatives/STYLE-GUIDE.md` Format A — torn red paper banner, white Cairo Bold hook, right-aligned checkmark bullets, red starburst badge (hero slide only), green WhatsApp CTA pill (final slide only), near-black footer strip, logo badge top-left (matches the rendered house examples in `social/creatives/`).
- **Evergreen constraint:** no dates, no countdown number, no "before Ramadan ends" language. Framing survives the full pre-Ramadan + through-Ramadan window.
- On-slide text is emoji-free (the composed-text fonts carry no emoji glyphs). Emojis live in the caption only.
