# Paid Meta/Instagram Carousel Ad — "اللحمة علينا والشوي عليك" (Grill/Mangal Occasion)

**Format:** Meta paid ad, 4-slide carousel
**Objective:** Drive WhatsApp orders for grill-ready cuts (mangal occasion — Friday/family gatherings, weekend grill days)
**Visual system:** Slots into `_templates/social-creatives/STYLE-GUIDE.md` Format A (torn paper hook banner, red starburst badge, green WhatsApp CTA pill, white trust icons)
**Status:** Copy ready for design. No prices included — see flags below before this ships.

---

## Slide 1 — Hook

**On-slide text (torn paper banner, dominant, Cairo Bold red-on-white):**

> اللحمة علينا 🥩 والشوي عليك 🔥

**Design note:** This is the phrase, tightened into two beats with a breath between them so it reads as a punchy exchange, not a sentence — the pause is doing the hook work. Emojis stand in for a visual pause; if the design already has strong iconography, the emojis can drop from the on-slide text and live only in the caption.

**Alt hook to test (craft/specificity angle, swap in for an A/B variant):**

> إحنا قطّعناها ليك... مالكش غير إنك تشعل النار 🔥
