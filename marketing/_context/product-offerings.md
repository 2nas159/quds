## Delivery & Payment Terms

- **Delivery areas:** Başakşehir, Esenyurt, Arnavutköy, Bahçeşehir (Istanbul European side)
- **Payment method:** Cash on delivery only
- **Free delivery threshold:** Orders over 5,000 TRY
- **Delivery fee (orders under 5,000 TRY):** 150 TRY
- **Order-to-delivery timing:** Same-day order typically delivered next day ("اطلب النهارده يوصلك طازج بكرة")
