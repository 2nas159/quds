# ملحمة القدس — Brand Content Guide

## Business Model
Dark Store / Cloud Butchery operating in Istanbul. No physical storefront. Orders are placed via WhatsApp, fulfilled fresh per order, and delivered directly to the customer's door. Payment is cash on delivery.

## Target Audience
Arabic-speaking community residing in Istanbul, concentrated in:
- Başakşehir
- Esenyurt
- Arnavutköy
- Bahçeşehir

## Core Messaging Pillars (use consistently across all content)

1. **توصيل سريع لباب بيتك** (Fast delivery to your doorstep)
2. **الدفع عند الاستلام** (Cash on delivery)
3. **طازج لباب بيتك** (Fresh to your doorstep)

These three pillars should appear, in some form, across ad copy, post captions, and comment replies. They directly address the brand's core challenge: the Trust Gap created by having no physical store.

## Hard Content Rule — NEVER VIOLATE

**NEVER mention or imply that products are sourced/bought from a market (e.g., Fatih market) in any public-facing content — posts, ad copy, comment replies, or customer messages.**

This includes:
- No direct statements ("بنجيبها من السوق")
- No indirect hints referencing market sourcing
- When asked about origin/quality, redirect to hygiene, control, and freshness — e.g., "بنذبح بنفسنا في مسلخ خاضع للرقابة والنظافة الكاملة" — without naming a market as the source.

## Trust Gap — The #1 Marketing Challenge

Because there is no physical store for customers to visit, all content must proactively compensate with transparency signals:
- Cash on delivery (removes payment risk)
- Freshness cues (visual, verbal)
- Hygiene and quality control messaging
- Real behind-the-scenes content (butchering, packaging) — without showing Enes's face, since he is not camera-comfortable

## Content Formats (priority order)
1. Reels / Stories
2. Product catalog carousel posts
3. Discount / promotional posts
