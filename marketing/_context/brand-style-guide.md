# ملحمة القدس — Brand Style Guide

## Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Red | `#b90f2a` | Backgrounds, buttons, headlines, price badges, brand accents |
| White | `#ffffff` | Text on red backgrounds, clean backgrounds, negative space |
| Near-Black | `#1a1a1a` | Body text on white/light backgrounds (secondary color) |

**Usage rules:**
- Primary red should dominate any badge, CTA button, or price callout.
- Never place red text on a red background — always pair red with white or near-black for contrast.
- Avoid introducing additional brand colors outside this palette unless explicitly approved.

## Typography

- **Headlines / Hooks:** Cairo Bold — used for all hook text, prices, and CTAs overlaid on images.
- **Body / Secondary text:** Cairo Regular or Tajawal — used for trust-signal lines, fine print, hashtags.
- Arabic text must always render right-to-left correctly; never left-align Arabic body text.

## Logo Usage

- Logo (sheep head + crossed cleavers icon, with "ملحمة القدس" wordmark) should appear in the **top-left corner** of every post/ad creative for consistency.
- Maintain clear space around the logo — do not let product imagery or text overlap it.
- Logo should be used in its white/red version depending on background contrast; never distort or recolor outside the brand palette.

## Standard Creative Template Elements

Every ad/post creative should include, in consistent positions:

| Element | Position |
|---|---|
| Logo | Top-left |
| Hook text (product name/benefit) | Overlaid near top or center, white text on dark badge |
| Price badge (if applicable) | Bottom-left, red starburst/badge shape |
| "اطلب الآن" CTA button | Bottom-center, green (WhatsApp brand color), pill-shaped |
| Trust icons (cash on delivery, delivery) | Bottom-right, white line icons |
| WhatsApp number | Bottom strip, small text |
| Delivery areas | Bottom strip, small text |

## Watermark Guidance

- A semi-transparent wordmark watermark may be used across the center of product photography for brand protection.
- **Do not let the watermark obscure or overlap the product itself** — reposition or reduce opacity if it cuts across the meat/product in a way that reduces visual clarity.

## Photography Style

- Close-up, high-detail shots of raw product (macro shots preferred to emphasize freshness and color).
- Natural lighting preferred to show authentic red tones of fresh meat.
- Occasional "behind the scenes" shots (hands, cutting board, packaging) are encouraged — never show Enes's face.
- Props: rosemary sprigs, citrus slices, peppers used sparingly for visual freshness cues — should not distract from the product itself.
